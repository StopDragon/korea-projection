G70 USB Wi-Fi Bridge 0.4.1 — experimental companion for Korea Projection

The bridge, log-export, build and test scripts are AGPL-3.0-only, as marked
in their source headers. COPYING contains the license. Their preferred source
and build instructions are in experimental/g70-usbwifi/ of the accompanying
Korea Projection source tree. Runtime shell/awk source is included directly
in each NEMESIS package. Korea Projection and HMG-API are separate dependencies.
This companion does not bundle or re-sign their APKs or wireless-mode packages.

88x2bu.ko is an unchanged, separately loaded GPL-2.0 kernel driver:
SHA-256 3e78ee38942e08667f68c287f0576ac21855c34cc516710de27cd49fa6137110
Realtek / cilynx/rtl88x2bu, upstream fc158d1f71b9e5dfcd1fccaf4d28725b65086e3b.
Vehicle build materials and GPL patches were obtained from:
https://github.com/MANN1309/ev6-kr-usbwifi-dongle/tree/3c4e0bb/pkg/source/driver
Those materials attribute the GPL vehicle driver adaptation to jmk-wifi 1.3.4:
https://github.com/JongMinKim-93/jmk-gen5w-ioniq5
The complete upstream driver source archive, applied patches, configuration,
build scripts, original notices and GPL COPYING are retained in source/driver/
inside the Bridge package (pkg/source/driver/ in the repository).

ctrl-status is an independently implemented, read-only UNIX datagram STATUS
client. Its MIT-0 source/license and the statically linked musl/compiler-runtime
license notices are in source/ (pkg/source/ in the repository). The ARM helper
and driver are unchanged from the vehicle-tested 0.4.0 companion.

The referenced authors have not endorsed this G70 companion. The 0.4.0 vehicle
test confirmed displayed CarPlay, DHCP and AP operation on the exact firmware
and USB ID in README.md. The owner reports frozen/unresponsive video while
music continues. The owner deliberately forced a disconnect to exit the frozen
screen. Session termination is not treated as proof of spontaneous link loss;
the cause of the display freeze remains unknown.
The 0.4.1 source port and stricter USB selection guards have host-test coverage
but have not yet been retested in the vehicle. See VALIDATION.md for scope.
