Korea Projection: StopDragon/korea-projection, AGPL-3.0. Base sources/assets are
the user-provided KP 1.7.4 and NX4HEV personal 1.7.4.1 bundles. Original signed
status APK, BusyBox and both signed wireless-mode archives remain unchanged.
The new dispatch/supervisor/build/test scripts are AGPL-3.0-only. COPYING is
included. Preferred runtime shell source is included directly in the package.
Exact input hashes and module pins are recorded in ORIGINS.json / RELEASE.json.

The previously supplied NX4 GRT binaries and unused EV6 controller/reference
modules are not redistributed by this integration. Their exact corresponding
GRT source was not established and no active runtime path uses them.
Original APK, BusyBox and signed wireless-mode inputs are supplied to prepare.py
by the builder, verified by SHA-256 and never re-signed here. The NX4 2.4.6 mode
remains a local input; its exact corresponding hooks source was not established
for public redistribution. No private package ZIP or its code is committed.
